Why Chuck

Executive public safety leader with results.

Chuck Dowd spent 34 years in executive public safety leadership, retiring as an Assistant Chief of the NYPD. He commanded critical operations including the nation's busiest 9-1-1 center, handling over 12 million calls annually and dispatching 4.5 million emergency runs per year.

He led 9-1-1 operations on September 11, 2001, through the 2003 Northeast Blackout, and during Hurricane Sandy. Today he lives in Sea Pines with his wife Madeline, a retired NYPD Detective, ready to bring that same crisis-tested leadership and technology-driven solutions to Ward 5.

Pillar 01

Public Safety

Proactive use of technology to maintain safe spaces and address problems before they occur.

  • Establish public safety policies that focus on Island priorities
  • Strategies that work to protect our island's roads and public spaces
  • Smart cameras, license plate readers, and real-time monitoring
02

Pillar 02

Smarter Transportation

Traffic management using real-time adaptive tech that solves gridlock.

  • Adaptive signal timing on key corridors to ease congestion
  • Alternative transportation solutions for Island residents and workforce
  • Continuous traffic monitoring to identify and fix bottlenecks
03

Pillar 03

Responsible Growth

Maintain our Island's character and culture through thoughtful growth.

  • Protect our island's unique tree canopy, waterway, and beach environments
  • Enforce our Town's policies that monitor short-term rentals
  • New projects must prove no harm to traffic, drainage, or emergency response

Pillar 04

Results-First Governance

Building a more collaborative Town Council with measurable outcomes.

  • Building a more collaborative Town Council
  • Establish a unity of purpose between the Town and County Councils
  • Tech tools to improve town responsiveness to residential and commercial needs
